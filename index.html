About the Lab

The Algorithm Innovation Laboratory (AI Lab) brings together researchers in computer science, computational biology, and data science to develop intelligent algorithms, high-performance analysis workflows, and data-driven methods that advance both fundamental discovery and applied research.

Under the leadership of Professor Yu-Feng Huang, the lab explores methods for next-generation sequencing, biomarker discovery, gene analysis, machine learning, and scientific computing. The goal is to translate computational innovation into practical tools and impactful research across biology, healthcare, and information technology.

Lab Focus

  • Big data analysis for next-generation sequencing (NGS)
  • Bioinformatics and computational biology
  • Data mining and machine learning
  • AI-driven solutions for scientific and medical data
